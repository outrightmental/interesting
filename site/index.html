the threshold

{{ worlds.worlds.length | words | capitalize }} small worlds, none of them the default, in one feed below that keeps going for as long as you do. One sideways question picks a first one to open.

Not everyone likes stars. So before this site offers anything it asks one short question, sideways, about a door or a stone or the rate at which you tap, and glances at your clock while you answer. It never asks you to report on yourself, it never asks the same way twice, and whatever it concludes fades over a few days. Answer in your persona above, or do not: every world is open either way, including the one it would never have picked for you.

Each world is built for a different frame of mind, named over its card. The mood atlas shows the whole apparatus: every way it asks and every answer it can arrive at, with a way to be asked again or forgotten.
