interesting

You have not been offered anything yet.

Not everyone likes stars. This site used to hand everybody the same night sky and hope, which is a poor way to treat a whole population. So it asks first — sideways, about a door or a stone or the rate at which you tap — and reads the clock and your time zone over your shoulder while you answer. The question is different on every arrival, you are never asked to report on yourself, and whatever it decides wears off.

the query

This one needs scripting. Without it, nothing is hidden from you: every world is listed further down, and the mood atlas describes each orientation and the question mechanisms behind it.
