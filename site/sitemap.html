Axiom. This site is written and rewritten by a model asked to make it more interesting as a single coherent whole, and interesting means one thing here: how long you stay engaged, how much you want to keep going, and how intrigued, astonished or entertained you are while you do. One rule stands over every one of those runs: all of the content stays reachable from the root, through the navigation on the home sky and through sitemap.xml. A page that arrives is wired into both in the same run, or the change is refused.
