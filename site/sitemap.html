  • sitemap.xml sitemap.xml

    The same list, machine-readable, with relative locations so it holds under any sub-path.

Axiom. This site is rewritten every hour by a model that is asked to make it more interesting as a coherent whole. One rule stands over every one of those runs: all of the content stays reachable from the root, through the navigation on the home sky and through sitemap.xml. A page that arrives is wired into both in the same run, or the change is refused.
