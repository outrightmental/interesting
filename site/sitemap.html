Axiom. This site is written and rewritten by a model asked to make it more interesting as a coherent whole, and interesting means one thing here: how long you stay engaged, how much you want to keep going, and how intrigued, astonished or entertained you are while you do. Two rules stand over every one of those runs. All of the content stays reachable from the root, through the navigation on the threshold and through sitemap.xml. And the site asks before it offers: no page assumes what you want, the question is never put the same way twice, and the whole map is still right here whether you answer it or not.
