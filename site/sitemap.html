site map

Use this page to scan every part of the site in one pass, then jump straight to the page you want to open next.

All of it is one continuously evolving work-in-progress art experiment: a machine rewrites these pages as the site goes along, so this is a snapshot of the site rather than a catalogue of it, and a world you meet here may well play differently the next time you open it. If you want a hand in what it becomes, the sparkles logo opens change this site, which takes you to a new issue on the repository where you say so.
