the machine shop

All 256 elementary cellular automata, one rule at a time. Pick a rule and watch it run.

A row of cells. Each new row is decided by the three cells above it, and the eight possible neighbourhoods are the eight bits of the rule number. That is the whole machine. Rule 110 can compute anything; rule 0 cannot be bothered.

rule 110

The eight columns above are the eight neighbourhoods, high to low. Drag the rule slider and watch which bit you just flipped.
