privacy

What you make here stays in your browser. The only thing measured is that a visit happened, and that only if you say yes.

what stays in your browser

Everything this site remembers about you is one JSON document in your own browser's storage: the stars of your persona, what the sideways question read of you, and whatever the worlds you have played with kept — a sealed capsule, an archived omen, a core sample. None of it is sent anywhere. There is no account, no profile and no server holding a copy.

Open state in the menu under the sparkles logo to read that document, copy it, paste someone else's over it, or throw it away. Throwing it away is immediate and complete: the site keeps no second copy to restore it from.

If your browser refuses storage — private browsing, or settings that allow none — the site holds the same document in memory instead, and it is gone when you close the page. Nothing breaks either way.

what the counter counts

If you accept analytics, this site loads Google Analytics 4, which counts visits and which pages hold attention. That is the whole of it: no advertising, no profiles sold on, nothing about you beyond what a visit looks like from the outside.

Nothing is loaded and no cookie is set before you accept. Decline and the tag is never fetched; accept now and decline later and its cookies are cleared. The choice lives behind cookies in the same menu, and it can be changed as often as you like.

what the host sees

The pages are plain files served from a content delivery network. Like any web host, it records the requests it answers — an address, a time, a path — to serve the files and keep itself standing. That record belongs to the host, is not joined to anything above, and is not something this site reads.

No fonts, trackers, embeds or third-party widgets are fetched from anywhere else. The cookie banner is a copy of an open-source library, served from this site like everything else.

what you can take back

All of it, at any moment, without asking anyone: decline cookies under cookies, and clear the document under state. There is nothing left over for you to request, because there is nowhere else for it to be.

If something here does not match what you find, say so with steer the site at the bottom of any page — that opens an issue on the repository this site is built from, in public, where it can be fixed. The other half of the fine print is terms.
