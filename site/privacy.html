privacy

Everything this site keeps of you is one small document in your own browser, and it goes nowhere else. Here is the whole of it, and how to take it back: state and cookies, both under the sparkles logo.

what stays in your browser

Everything this site remembers about you is one document in your browser's storage: the stars of your persona, what the sideways question read of you, a tally of the puzzles you have solved and the tries and hints they took, and whatever else the worlds you played with kept. None of it is sent anywhere. There is no account or server holding a copy.

Open state under the sparkles logo to read that document, copy it, paste someone else's over it, or throw it away. Throwing it away is complete: the site keeps no second copy to restore it from.

If your browser refuses storage, the site holds the same document in memory instead. It is gone when you close the page.

what analytics counts

If you accept analytics, this site loads Google Analytics 4, which counts visits and which pages hold attention. There is no advertising and no profile sold on.

Nothing is loaded and no cookie is set before you accept. Decline and the tag is never fetched; accept and then decline and its cookies are cleared. Change that choice under cookies in the same menu.

what the host sees

The pages are files served from a content delivery network. Like any web host, it records the requests it answers to serve the files. That record belongs to the host and is not joined to anything above.

No fonts, embeds or third-party widgets are fetched from anywhere else. The cookie banner is served from this site.

what you can take back

Decline under cookies and remove the document under state. If something here does not match what you find, use change this site under the sparkles logo. The other half of the fine print is terms.
