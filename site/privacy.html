privacy

What this site keeps of you is one small document in your own browser, which goes nowhere else, and, only if you say yes to journeys, one random number beside it. Here is the whole of it, how the site counts how far its pages are read, what the analytics and the ads you can accept do, and how to take it all back: state and cookies, both under the sparkles logo.

what stays in your browser

Everything this site remembers about you is one document in your browser's storage: the stars of your persona, what the sideways question read of you, a tally of the puzzles you have solved and the tries and hints they took, and whatever else the worlds you played with kept. None of it is sent anywhere. There is no account or server holding a copy.

Open state under the sparkles logo to read that document, copy it, paste someone else's over it, or throw it away. Throwing it away is complete: the site keeps no second copy to restore it from.

If your browser refuses storage, the site holds the same document in memory instead. It is gone when you close the page.

how far each page is read

The studio that makes this site, Outright Mental, counts its own pages with Signal, its own analytics. Every page carries a column of tiny invisible images, one every 200 pixels down it and one at its foot, which your browser fetches from Signal as you scroll near them. Each one adds one to a count for that page and that depth on that day, and that is all that is kept: no cookie, no identifier and nothing stored in your browser, and Signal's server keeps nothing of the request but the count, so it cannot tell one visitor from another or follow anyone. It works without any script and asks nobody's leave, because it keeps nothing about you, only about the page.

journeys, only if you say yes

The cookie banner also asks about journeys. Say yes and this browser keeps a random number in its own storage, apart from the document above, and sends it with each page you open, so the studio can see how people find their way between its sites and games: a link from here to another of them carries the number in the part of its address no server sees, and the next site joins it to its own only if you said yes there too. An email address you give any of the studio's sites joins you there in the same way. Signal keeps the number and any address only as a keyed hash, never as themselves.

Nothing of this is kept or sent until you say yes. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control, that is taken as a no, whatever you answer. Say no later, under cookies, and the number is erased from your browser and you are erased from Signal on every one of the studio's sites at once.

what analytics counts

If you accept analytics, this site loads Google Analytics 4, which counts visits and which pages hold attention. Nothing it counts is sold on, and no profile of you is made from it here.

Nothing is loaded and no cookie is set before you accept. Decline and the tag is never fetched; accept and then decline and its cookies are cleared. Change that choice under cookies in the same menu.

what the ads are

When the cookie banner offers advertising and you accept it, the worlds show ads from Clickadu, an ad network: a banner under the puzzle and, now and then, a small notification-style card in a corner. Nothing else: no pop-up, no pop-under, no ad that covers the page, and none on the threshold, the mood atlas, the site map or these two pages of fine print.

Clickadu chooses and counts those ads from its own servers, and may set cookies of its own to do it; what it does with them is in Clickadu's privacy policy. The site passes it nothing but which zone to fill; its code does run in the page, as any ad's does, which is why it waits for your yes. Decline and that code is never fetched; accept and then decline and the page reloads without it. Clickadu's own cookies are then for your browser to clear, because a site can only clear cookies of its own.

what the host sees

The pages are files served from a content delivery network. Like any web host, it records the requests it answers to serve the files. That record belongs to the host and is not joined to anything above.

Apart from Signal's images, and Google Analytics and Clickadu's ads only once you accept them, nothing is fetched from anywhere else: no fonts, embeds or widgets. The cookie banner and Signal's own script are served from this site.

what you can take back

Decline analytics, ads and journeys under cookies and remove the document under state; the studio's own privacy notice is at outrightmental.com/privacy. If something here does not match what you find, use change this site under the sparkles logo. The other half of the fine print is terms.
