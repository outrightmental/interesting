terms

Play with the worlds and keep what you make. Take the readings as a game and not as advice, and your browser as a sketchbook and not as a vault.

what this is

A collection of worlds, each a small rite you can play through, whose pages, words and puzzles can change. A thing you liked may be different or gone when you return. Nothing here is a product, a service or a promise of one.

what you can do with it

Look around, play with everything, place as many stars as you like, and take what you make with you — a reading, a copy of what your browser kept, a postcard or a sigil. What you make is yours. You can share a copy of what your browser kept with someone else.

The words, pictures and code behind the site belong to whoever wrote them; nothing on this page grants you a licence to them. Do not use the site to attack it, to attack anyone through it, or to pretend it says something it does not.

what it is not

Not advice. The readings are invented from your answers and the clock. They are for amusement: nothing here knows anything about you, diagnoses anything, or should be weighed in a decision that matters.

Not storage you can trust. What the site keeps is in your browser, which can remove it at any time, and nothing here is backed up (privacy has the detail). Keep your own copy of anything you would be sorry to lose.
