terms

A thought experiment you are welcome to play with. It promises nothing, and neither does anyone behind it.

what this is

A small collection of interactive worlds that rewrites itself: the pages, the words and the toys change as the site is iterated on, and a thing you liked may be different or gone the next time you look. That is the experiment, not a fault in it. Nothing here is a product, a service or a promise of one.

what you can do with it

Look at it, play with everything, place as many stars as you like, and take what you make with you — a reading, an exported state document, a postcard, a sigil. What you make is yours. Sharing a state document with someone else is the site working as intended.

The words, pictures and code behind the site belong to whoever wrote them; nothing on this page grants you a licence to them. Do not use the site to attack it, to attack anyone through it, or to pretend it says something it does not.

what it is not

Not advice. The readings are invented on the spot from whatever you answered and whatever the clock says, and they are for your amusement: nothing here knows anything about you, diagnoses anything, or should be weighed in a decision that matters.

Not storage you can trust. Everything the site keeps is in your own browser, which can clear it at any time, and nothing here is backed up anywhere (privacy has the detail). Keep your own copy of anything you would be sorry to lose.

no warranty, and no liability

This site is offered exactly as it is, with no warranty of any kind — express or implied, including fitness for any purpose, availability or accuracy. To the fullest extent the law allows, nobody who made, hosts or contributes to it is liable for any loss or damage of any kind arising from your use of it, and you hold them harmless for it. If you are not happy with that, the remedy is to close the tab.

if something is wrong

Say so. Steer the site at the bottom of any page opens an issue on the repository this site is built from; that is the whole of the complaints department, and it is public. Anything that has to be taken down can be asked for there.
