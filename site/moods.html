how it asks

It never asks you to report on yourself. It asks about a door, a stone, the thing you would put in your pocket, the rate at which you tap, how long you hold a button down, where you put a single mark in an empty field. It reads the clock and your time zone over your shoulder while you answer, and it knows how long it has been since you were last here. You get a different mechanism on arrival each time — the last few are remembered precisely so that they are not repeated — and the library below is meant to keep growing.

  • The mechanisms are listed here once scripting is available. Without it, nothing is lost: every world below is a plain link.

what it is choosing between

Fourteen orientations, each with one world behind it. Several of those worlds are the night sky this site grew up as; the rest are not sky at all, which is the point. Nothing in this list is the default, and the site would rather you overruled it than took its word.

  • banked low wants less asked of it the quiet room
  • wound tight wants to shove something and watch it go the kinetic floor
  • cold and clear wants a mechanism to take apart the machine shop
  • low and slow wants something that grows downward loam
  • full of half-sentences wants words put in the fire the word kiln
  • magpie wants a strange specimen in a drawer the apocrypha desk
  • looking up wants distance and scale the wish constellation
  • wants a rite wants to light something on purpose the lantern ritual
  • in the long look wants to re-read its own record the diary
  • weather coming wants pressure, front and squall the weather lab
  • ears first wants to listen to something decay the echo chamber
  • minding something wants a living thing to keep the terrarium
  • asking elsewhere wants an answer it did not author the archive oracle
  • after symmetry wants a pattern to close the orbital weaver

Axiom. This site asks before it offers. No page assumes what a visitor wants, the query is never the same way twice, and nothing here is ever a gate: the whole site is reachable from the threshold and from sitemap.xml with the question ignored, declined, or scripting switched off entirely. What the site learns is partly remembered and wears off.
