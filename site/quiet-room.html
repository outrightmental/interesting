the quiet room

A breath to follow and a dimmer that stays where you leave it. Nothing here keeps score.

settling

The ring takes six seconds out and six back. Follow it or do not. The dimmer is real: it darkens this room only, and it stays where you leave it.

The room is lit low and the door is shut.

Nothing is required of you here.
There is no score, no streak and no next thing.

Press and hold the ring, or the spacebar, and the room holds its breath with you.
